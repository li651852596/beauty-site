About KYHS Beauty: False Eyelashes & Mink Lashes Manufacturer

Reliable manufacturing, flexible customization and export experience — focused on false lashes and beauty accessories.

Who We Are

Part of the KYHS Export Group

KYHS Beauty is the dedicated eyelash and beauty-accessories line of KEYUE HONGSHENG I/E CO., LTD. We manufacture false eyelashes, lash tools and private-label packaging for beauty brands, salons and retailers across Europe, North America, Australia and beyond.

We focus on accessories and tools rather than formula cosmetics — a category with simpler compliance requirements that lets our customers bring products to market faster.

Our product range covers mink lashes, faux mink, synthetic and 3D lashes, professional eyelash extensions, tweezers and applicators, makeup brushes and private-label packaging. Whether you are launching a new lash brand or expanding a salon supply line, we can develop custom curls, lengths and volumes to match your market and put your logo on bands, boxes and retail sets.

Because false eyelashes are classified as beauty accessories in most markets, they generally avoid the CPNP registration required for cosmetics in the EU and the FDA MoCRA registration in the US. That simpler compliance path means lower barriers and faster time to shelf for importers, distributors and online sellers.
