FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for importers, lash brands and salon owners.

Are false eyelashes considered cosmetics? Do they need cosmetic registration?

False eyelashes are classified as beauty accessories rather than formula cosmetics in most markets. Unlike mascara or skincare, they generally do not require CPNP registration in the EU or FDA MoCRA registration in the US, making them much simpler to import and sell.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

We keep flexible, low minimums so new brands can test the market. MOQ depends on the lash style and packaging; contact us on WhatsApp with your target quantity and we will confirm the exact figure.

Can I put my own logo and packaging on the lashes?

Yes. We provide full OEM/ODM and private-label service: custom logo on bands and boxes, your artwork, and retail-ready packaging. Send your design or ask our team to create one for you.

How long does sampling and production take?

A digital proof is typically ready within 24 hours, and physical samples within 5-7 working days. Mass production begins after sample approval; exact lead time depends on quantity and customization.

Which lash styles can you manufacture?

We produce mink and faux mink lashes, synthetic and 3D/5D volume styles, professional extension trays, and matching tools and packaging. Custom curls, lengths and thicknesses are available on request.
