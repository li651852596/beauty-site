Compliance Note

Why accessories are easier to source

Formula cosmetics (mascara, eyeshadow, skincare) require CPNP registration in the EU and FDA MoCRA registration in the US. False lashes, tweezers, brushes and packaging are accessories — a much simpler compliance path for importers and brands.
